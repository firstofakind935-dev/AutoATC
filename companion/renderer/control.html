Set up position tracking

Draws a transparent overlay directly on top of your game, so you can line up regions and calibration clicks precisely on real screen pixels - even with a single monitor.

Show the overlay

Pick the monitor your game is on (windowed or borderless - a game in exclusive fullscreen can't be overlaid), then show the overlay. It's transparent and click-through until you arm an action below.

Overlay not shown

Define regions

Click a button, then drag a box directly on your game (through the overlay) over the matching HUD element. Only needs to be redone if you move or resize the game window.

4

Calibrate the map

Pick two airports you can currently see on your minimap, then click each one's exact spot directly on your game (through the overlay), in order. Re-calibrate any time you pan or zoom the minimap.
