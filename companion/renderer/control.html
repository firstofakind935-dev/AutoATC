Set up position tracking

Draws a transparent overlay directly on top of your game, so you can line up regions and calibration clicks precisely on real screen pixels - even with a single monitor.

Settings

2

Show the overlay

Pick the monitor your game is on (windowed or borderless - a game in exclusive fullscreen can't be overlaid), then show the overlay. It's transparent and click-through until you arm an action below.

Overlay not shown
3

Define regions

Click a button, then drag a box directly on your game (through the overlay) over the matching HUD element. Only needs to be redone if you move or resize the game window.

4

Position fix

Best: open the big map in the game, select its region above once, then press F8 (or the button). The companion finds your green aircraft marker and works out where the map is looking by itself - no airports to click, any zoom or pan. While tracking it re-fixes on its own whenever the big map is open.

No map fix yet

Minimap (automatic): select the minimap region above. When you tick the box below, the companion matches the minimap against the world map every few seconds and keeps your position right, using your marker. A match that jumps somewhere unlikely is ignored unless it is very strong, so dead reckoning (heading + speed) and your stand fix carry you over open sea.

No minimap fix yet

On a stand? Pick the airport, type your stand number and press the button. You're put exactly on that stand's line on the ground chart, and your heading is checked against the line so a wrong number is caught.
