    Let the autopilot press keys?

    In LIVE mode the autopilot presses your configured keys in whatever window has keyboard focus. Keep PTFS focused while it flies.

    Roblox's rules can treat outside software that automates gameplay as a violation. You use this at your own risk to your account.

    You can switch back to DRY RUN at any time. In DRY RUN the autopilot only shows what it would press.
