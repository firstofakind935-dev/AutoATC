AutoATC Companion

Reads your own game window (never anyone else's) to estimate your aircraft's position and reports it to your fleet's monitor service. Nothing here talks to Discord directly.

1. Settings

2. Pick the game window

3. Define regions (once per session/window size)

Click a "Select" button, then drag a box on the video over the matching part of your HUD. Do this once after picking the window; you don't need to redo it unless you resize the game window.

4. Calibrate the map

Pick two airports you can currently see on your minimap, then click each one's exact spot on the minimap in order. Re-do this any time you pan or zoom the minimap - the calibration is only valid for the current view.
